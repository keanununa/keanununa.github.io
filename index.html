Method

A result is useful
even when it says no.

  1. 01

    State the object

    Separate what is being measured from the proxy used to observe it.

  2. 02

    Fix the failure condition

    Decide what evidence would change the claim before seeing the result.

  3. 03

    Keep the negative result

    A failed hypothesis narrows the search space and belongs in the record.
